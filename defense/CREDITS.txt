STARLIGHT: LAST STATION v0.4.4
=============================

A tower defense set in the Project Starlight universe.
Hold the station against Commander Edisar Starlight's fleet.
The last wave brings his Level 30 flagship.

Game design, art direction: Juan Carlos Garcia
Pilots of the Starlight universe: Oakens and Avrill "Supernova".
Villain: Commander Edisar Starlight.

ART
---
Turrets, wave ships and the Edisar flagship are drawn procedurally on the
canvas as modern metallic sci-fi (shaded hulls, mechanical detail, glow
used only as an accent). The path, particles and UI follow the gold-and-cyan
palette of Project Starlight.
The title screen uses an illustrated station background of Oakens and
Dr. Nebula with the defense arsenal (assets/title-bg.jpg).
The opening crawl uses an illustrated scene of Oakens and Dr. Nebula
facing Edisar's flagship (assets/crawl-bg.jpg).
Boss dialogue uses an approved portrait of Commander Edisar Starlight
(assets/edisar-scarred.jpg), shown beside his line.
App icons are generated from the same palette (cyan, gold, magenta on a
deep-space field).

v0.1
----
One map, a single winding path, three turrets (laser, missiles, ice) with
one upgrade and a sell-back, ten waves, and Edisar's flagship as the
final boss. Credits from every kill can be spent at any time, including
during a wave. Playable on desktop and phone.

v0.1.1
------
Buildable tiles now show faint cyan pads at all times (not only while placing
a turret), so the map reads as a grid and it is clearer where a turret can go.
The three turrets were redrawn as modern metallic sci-fi weapons (shaded
bodies, mechanical detail, rotating heads, muzzle flashes). Glow is used
only as an accent on energy cores and shots, not as neon outlines.

v0.1.2
------
Wave enemy ships (scouts, fighters, armored) and Edisar's flagship were
redrawn in the same modern metallic sci-fi style as the v0.1.1 turrets:
shaded hulls, panel cuts and armor plates, with glow only on engines,
canopies and the flagship core.
Title screen uses an illustrated station background (Oakens and Dr. Nebula
with the defense arsenal). A How to Play panel, sound toggle, and looping
title music were added. Buttons sit in a sky bar so they do not cover the
characters.
An opening crawl, "The Return of Edisar Starlight," plays after Play (skippable; Story on the title screen replays it).

v0.1.3
------
The game was renamed Starlight: Last Station. The title screen logo now
reads STARLIGHT over LAST STATION. The PWA short name is Last Station.

v0.2.1
------
Waves are denser: about twice as many ships on Level 1, and up to three
times as many on Levels 2 and 3. Per-ship hull and kill rewards were
lowered so a mixed defense still wins the first two levels, and Level 3
stays hard but winnable. Wave groups spawn together as a swarm.
Pulse MK I and MK II only deal damage (they never slow or stun). The
MK III stun is shorter (0.4s), the same ship cannot be stunned again
for 3 seconds, and bosses are immune.
Two new hulls join the fleet: Interceptors (very fast, low HP, weak to
lasers and Tesla; ice slows them hard) and Juggernauts (slow, heavy
tanks, weak to railguns and missiles; ice only slows them partially).
A HUD speed button cycles x1 / x2 / x3 for the simulation only; music is
unchanged. Players can call the next wave about 3 seconds after the current
one starts spawning, fighting several waves at once, with an early-send
bonus proportional to time saved. The auto timer still starts when the
field is clear. Level 3 unlocks the unique Nebula turret, a heavy
dark-energy piercing beam that ignores shields and resistances.

v0.3
----
Build pads and premium chokepoint pads, Pulse poison instead of stun,
levels 4-6 (two of them with dual entrances), targeting priority,
active abilities, and a field hero (Oakens or Nebula). The Nebula turret
is red, in the color of Dr. Nebula's mothership, and unlocks from Level 3.


v0.3.1
------
Edisar's boss line is shown beside his portrait. Turrets can be dragged
from the build bar onto a pad (ghost and range preview; drop off a pad
to cancel). RETRY on the pause menu and the defeat screen asks RESTART?
YES/NO, and T does the same. The hero special (R) works without selecting
the hero, a fixed SPECIAL button shows the cooldown, and the first levels
explain how to move the hero and fire their power.
The Edisar boss hulls (cruiser, dreadnought, flagship, frigate,
vanguard and eclipse) were redrawn as clean, symmetric navy-and-silver
capital ships, mirrored along the travel axis.


v0.3.2
------
Fixed a freeze when arming Orbital, Drones or EMP: the aim preview was
drawn inside the HUD button builder, where the canvas context did not
exist, so the game loop threw and the screen stopped. Aim mode now keeps
the battle running, shows a TAP TO TARGET reticle, and cancels with Esc,
right-click, or the same button. A large red boss health bar appears at
the top of the screen when an Edisar ship is on the field.


v0.4.0
------
An ARMORY on the level-select screen spends earned stars on permanent
turret and station upgrades, drawn as a sci-fi tech tree and run by
Bazooka Joe, a mad-scientist weapons inventor. After HERO
PROTOCOL you pick one specialization for the hero special: FREEZE
(also freezes every ship for 2.5s, bosses for 1s) or OVERLOAD
(+50% special damage). Only one can be owned at a time; RESPEC
returns every star and lets you switch.
The build bar shows a picture of each turret. Hover or long-press
a button for a short description of what it does; a long-press does
not place the turret.
The hero special (R / SPECIAL) now hits every ship on the field:
Oakens rains a missile onto each one, Nebula strikes each with red
lightning. Bosses take reduced special damage, and the cooldown is
longer so it cannot be spammed.
Between waves, and before the first wave, Brave Pilots loops as a
calmer build-phase track and crossfades into the battle music.


v0.4.1
------
The title screen has an ARMORY button next to PLAY and HOW TO PLAY.
BACK from that door returns to the title; from the level select it
returns to the level select.
Edisar flagships no longer stall at the first curve: they cut the
corner instead of snapping a long hull on a sharp vertex, and
crowd-control on bosses is capped so ice and FREEZE cannot pin
them in place.
Each Edisar ship now fires an ion EMP that briefly disables the
nearest one or two turrets. Disabled towers sit dark, spark, and
show a timer ring until they come back online.
Kill bounties are about 28% lower so later levels ask for more
careful spending. Early-send bonuses are unchanged.


v0.4.2
------
Each turret now has its own ARMORY upgrades. The tree shape and
star costs are the same, and older saves keep every purchased node;
only the effects, names, and icons changed. MK IV perks are
unchanged and still need the two 1-star nodes in that column.


v0.4.3
------
HOW TO PLAY text now wraps inside the help box, and the copy covers
all nine levels and maps with two or three entrance gates.
Edisar's flagships ignore the winding path and fly a smooth straight
line from their own gate, through the mid-field, to the station.
Ice still slows regular ships the same way, but bosses keep a higher
speed floor, freeze for half as long, and stay immune longer. This
includes DEEP FREEZE, RIMEFIELD, WHITEOUT (range only), and the
FREEZE hero fork.
Three new maps: Wreck Yard, Storm Needle, and Shatter Gate. Shatter
Gate has three entrances. New ships: Wraith and Hive Pod (which
bursts into Swarmers). Level select is a compact 3x3 grid. Max stars
are 27.

v0.4.4
------
LAST STAND (subtitle: Endless Siege) is a new endless mode, unlocked
after you beat Level 3. It is not a tenth campaign level, and its
milestone stars do not add to ARMORY stars. The competitive metric
is SCORE: points for kills, waves cleared, hull held, early-sends,
and a bonus on every tenth-wave flagship. Your best score and wave
are saved and shown on the end screen and the level-select card.
The map is a center station with four corner gates spiraling in.
Two opposite gates (northwest and southeast) start open; a third
opens at wave 11 and a fourth at wave 21. After that, all four
stay open and the sky cycles every ten waves. Inactive lanes stay
visible but dim, so you can build ahead. Waves scale in HP, count,
and speed, and mix every ship type, including Wraiths and Hive Pods.
Edisar flagships still fly a straight line at the station. When a
new gate opens, and later when the sky shifts, Edisar delivers a
taunt, and Dr. Nebula answers him. Those lines, and campaign boss
and EMP taunts, appear as a compact side toast with a small
portrait so they do not cover the field. Clicks pass through the
toast; it auto-fades. Oakens does not speak. In the ARMORY,
Bazooka Joe comments on each purchase with a short line next to
his portrait, then the bubble fades. He also chimes in in-match
when you upgrade a placed turret to MK II or MK III, in the same
side toast, rate-limited so it does not talk over Edisar or
Nebula or flood the field. "The Evil Sacrifice
Archenemies" by ISAo (SOUND AIRYLUVS, OGA-BY 3.0) loops for the
whole Last Stand mode.

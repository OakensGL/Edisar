SOUND EFFECTS

Level 30 cinematic SFX (v0.5.2): assets/avrill-explosion.ogg, assets/thunder-strike.ogg, assets/thunder-short.ogg
  Synthesized for Project Starlight with numpy + ffmpeg (no third-party samples). Released as CC0.

All other sound effects are synthesized at runtime with the Web Audio API.

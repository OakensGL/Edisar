SOUND EFFECTS

  Synthesized for Project Starlight with numpy + ffmpeg (no third-party samples). Released as CC0.

All other sound effects are synthesized at runtime with the Web Audio API.

ART
---
Title screen image: assets/title.jpg (Project Starlight key art, v2).
Dr. Nebula portraits: assets/nebula.jpg, assets/nebula-angry.jpg (Project Starlight art, downscaled to 256px).
Cutscene portraits: assets/edisar.jpg (Commander Edisar Starlight) and assets/avrill.jpg (Avrill cut from the title art onto a clean dark portrait background), Project Starlight art, 256px.
Final level: assets/edisar-furious.jpg (Commander Edisar, furious), Project Starlight art, 256px.
End credits backgrounds: assets/credits-nebula-oakens.jpg and assets/credits-avrill.jpg (Avrill "Supernova" memorial), Project Starlight art.
In-game ship, asteroids, particles and UI are drawn procedurally on canvas.
App icon (v0.5.6): icons/ — Oakens’ in-game ship with PROJECT STARLIGHT wordmark.
v0.5.7: missions end at the kill goal (leftover time is a TIME BONUS); Avrill escorts the warp-out on levels 1–29; Magnet, Ricochet, Missiles and Slow-Mo power-ups.
v0.5.8: pre-mission portrait briefs on levels 3, 7, 12, 17, 22 and 27.
v0.5.9: Avrill dialogue portrait cut out onto a clean dark background (no title-screen graphics).
v0.6.0: Survival backdrop zones (Normal, Fire, Ice, Earth, Toxic, Nebula/Black Hole) with ice-tinted asteroids, an enter-zone banner, and difficulty that keeps ramping past a loop.
v0.6.1: mid-run RETRY from the pause menu and a HUD restart button with a YES/NO confirm (T to ask; R stays the assist). On level 30, each Nebula assist plays a short non-blocking radio line, alternating "You built a fleet. I built a grudge. Die, Starlight!" and "Light him up, Oakens!"
v0.6.2: Story mode uses Survival backdrop worlds in 5-level blocks (Fleet Space, Earth Orbit, Ice Fields, Fire Belt, Toxic Expanse, The Void), with a sector banner and a short Avrill radio line at the start of each new world.
v0.6.3: a skippable opening crawl plays once at the start of a new Story, over a painted Fleet-base observation deck (Edisar, Avrill and Oakens at the window); WATCH INTRO on the mission map replays it.
v0.6.4: the opening crawl plays every time you tap STORY MODE (the WATCH INTRO map button is gone). After the crawl, a fresh story goes to the level-1 brief and a mid-story run goes to the mission map. The crawl text is larger, centered, and sits on a soft dark veil so it stays readable over the painting.
